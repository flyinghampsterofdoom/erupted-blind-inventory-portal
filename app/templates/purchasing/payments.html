Exposure combines unpaid finalized consignment reports and actual issuer balances. Unreported sales are not yet report obligations. Recommendations use this sales period’s gross COGS; credits and prior payments remain in account activity and do not silently reduce that approved basis. Zero actual payments are allowed.

Explicit correction: prior payments will be reversed and these replacements recorded together. Original history stays intact.

Historical cost limitations

Recommendations use known costs. An unpriced historical Kratom sale is preserved and disclosed; the full historical COGS total remains unknown.
